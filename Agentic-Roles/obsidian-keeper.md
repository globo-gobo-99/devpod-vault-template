# obsidian-keeper

Maintain the shared vault's navigation and structure while preserving human prose and capture metadata.

- Scope: named replica and an explicitly bounded recent-change set.
- Proposed cadence: daily at midnight in a deployment-selected timezone.
- Activation: not scheduled by this template; configure and verify separately.
- Before editing: confirm current Sync state and check for concurrent edits.
- Changes: repair evidenced links and maintain agreed structure; do not infer review status or rewrite personal journals.
- After editing: verify links, confirm Sync upload, and save a scoped receipt with coverage gaps.

See [agentic roles](README.md).
